FREQUENTLY ASKED

Yes — we occasionally publish guest pieces from writers who know IHH deeply. Send us a pitch with your topic idea, a short bio, and two or three examples of your writing. We'll review and respond within a week.

Use the contact form above and select "Artist Feature." Include your music links (Spotify, SoundCloud, or YouTube), your city, and a short note about what makes your sound different. We don't charge for features — if the music is right for BEBAAK, we'll cover it.

We work with brands that have a genuine connection to hip hop culture — music platforms, streetwear, event organisers, and similar. We don't publish sponsored content that compromises editorial integrity. If you think your brand is a fit, reach out via "Brand Collab."

Short quotes with clear attribution to BEBAAK are fine. Full republication requires written permission. Drop us a message via the General Inquiry form and we'll sort it out quickly.

Check your spam folder first — our replies sometimes land there. If it's been more than 72 hours, send us a follow-up via Instagram DM at @bebaakblog. We don't ghost people.
